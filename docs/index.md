# Milli

Milli is a private, offline-first money tracker for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch: log what
you spend in a couple of taps, sort it with a colour-coded category wheel, and see where
the month is going.

Everything you enter stays on your device, and — only if you turn on iCloud sync — in your
own private iCloud account, which we cannot access. There is no Milli server, no account,
no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Milli Premium is a one-time purchase, not a
subscription.
