# Milli Support

**Last updated:** 16 September 2026

Milli is a private, offline-first personal finance tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch.

## Contact

The fastest way to reach us is email:

**milli.app.support@gmail.com**

We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. To help us help you faster, please include:

- your iPhone model and iOS version (**Settings → General → About**)
- your Apple Watch model and watchOS version, if the issue is on the watch
  (**Watch app → General → About**)
- the Milli version, shown in the app's settings
- what you expected to happen, and what happened instead

## Frequently asked questions

### I paid for Premium but my features are locked

Open the Milli Premium screen and tap **Restore Purchases**. Milli Premium is a
one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account, so it restores on any device signed
in with the same account, at no extra cost.

If restoring does not work, make sure you are signed into the App Store with the
same Apple Account you used to buy, then email us.

### What does Milli Premium include?

A single one-time payment — no subscription — that unlocks:

- **Unlimited accounts** — track as many accounts and ledgers as you need
- **Unlimited categories** — organise spending exactly how you think about it
- **Smart tags** — filter and group transactions beyond categories
- **Backup & iCloud sync** — keep your data safe and on all your devices
- **Home screen widgets** — see your balances without opening the app
- **Apple Watch app** — add and track spending right from your wrist

### How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple, so we cannot issue refunds ourselves.
Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### Is my financial data private?

Yes. Milli has no server, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party SDKs. Your
data stays on your device, and — only if you turn on iCloud sync — in your own
private iCloud account, which we cannot access. See the
[Privacy Policy](../privacy/) for full detail.

### My data is not syncing between devices

Check that:

1. Both devices are signed into the **same Apple Account**
2. **Settings → your name → iCloud → iCloud Drive** is on
3. iCloud sync is enabled in Milli's own settings
4. Both devices have a working internet connection

Sync happens in the background and can take a few minutes. Opening the app on the
second device usually prompts an immediate check.

### How do I back up or move my data?

Milli can export a backup file from its settings. Keep it somewhere safe — once
exported, that file is under your control, so treat it like a bank statement.

### Can I use Milli on iPad?

Milli is currently designed for iPhone only. It will run on an iPad in compatibility
mode, but the layout is not optimised for the larger screen.

### How do I turn off the Face ID / passcode lock?

Open the app's settings and disable the app lock in the security section. You will
be asked to authenticate once before the change takes effect.

### Exchange rates look wrong

Milli does not fetch live rates — it makes no network requests at all, by design.
Exchange rates are the ones you enter yourself, so you can update them in settings
whenever you want.

### I found a bug / I have a feature request

Please email **milli.app.support@gmail.com**. Bug reports with the details listed
under [Contact](#contact) above are the most useful, and feature requests are
genuinely read.

## Apple Watch

### How do I install Milli on my Apple Watch?

The watch app is a companion to the iPhone app, so it installs with it. Open the
**Watch** app on your iPhone, scroll to **Available Apps**, and tap **Install**
next to Milli. If automatic app install is turned on, it will already be there.

The Apple Watch app is part of Milli Premium. Until Premium is unlocked, the
watch app shows a locked screen with no purchase button — purchases are made on
the iPhone, then carried across to the watch automatically.

### What can I do on the watch?

- See **today's net** for the account in view — what today's transactions add up
  to, which is deliberately not the account's lifetime balance
- See **today's transactions** for that account
- **Add an expense or income**: turn the Digital Crown to set the amount, or tap
  the amount to type it on a keypad, pick a category, then tap **Save**
- **Switch accounts**: tap the account icon at the top of the screen

The watch shows only today, and only a handful of the most recent entries. It is
a glance and a quick entry point, not the full ledger.

### Can I use the watch away from my iPhone?

Yes. The watch keeps the last numbers your iPhone sent, so it opens on real
figures rather than a spinner when the phone is out of range, and anything you
enter is queued until the phone is reachable again.

The entry is saved to your ledger by the iPhone, though, so it is not final until
the two are back in touch. Bring the phone in range and open Milli on it to
settle anything waiting.

### An entry I added on my watch is greyed out

Grey means the iPhone has not confirmed it yet — the entry is queued, not lost.
Bring your iPhone within range with Bluetooth or Wi-Fi on, and open Milli on it.
The entry turns solid once it has been booked.

Queued entries can take a few minutes to go over on their own, because watchOS
decides when to deliver them. Opening Milli on both devices at once is the
fastest way to flush them.

### An entry disappeared from my watch instead of saving

That happens when the account or category it was filed under no longer exists on
the iPhone — usually because it was deleted there while the watch was out of
range holding older information. There is nothing for the iPhone to file the
entry against, so the watch drops it rather than leaving a row that could never
save. Re-enter it against a category that still exists.

### My watch says "Apple Watch is Premium" but I already bought it

The watch cannot check your purchase itself — it is told about it by your iPhone.
Open Milli on your iPhone with the watch nearby, and the lock lifts within a few
seconds. A watch that has never heard from its iPhone always starts locked,
because an unknown purchase is not treated as a granted one.

If it stays locked, confirm Premium is active on the iPhone first (tap **Restore
Purchases** if not), then open the app on the iPhone once more.

### My watch shows a dash instead of an amount

A dash means the watch has not received anything from your iPhone yet — Milli
will not invent a zero, because an empty day and an unsynced watch are not the
same thing. Open Milli on your iPhone with the watch nearby to send the first
update.

### My watch and iPhone are showing different accounts

That is intentional. The account you pick on the watch is the watch's own choice
and does not change what the iPhone has open, so you can log against a second
ledger from your wrist without rearranging the phone. Tap the account icon at the
top of the watch screen to change it.

If the account you picked on the watch is later deleted on the iPhone, the watch
falls back to whichever account the iPhone has open.

### The watch is showing old numbers

Open the app on your iPhone with the watch nearby. The watch asks for a fresh
update whenever it comes to the foreground and whenever the iPhone comes back
into range, so this normally resolves itself within a few seconds of the two
being together.

If it does not, check that Bluetooth is on, that the two devices are still
paired in the **Watch** app, and that Milli is installed on both.

### The watch app is in the wrong language

The watch follows the language set inside Milli on your iPhone, not the watch's
own system language. Change it in Milli's settings on the iPhone, and the watch
picks it up with its next update.
